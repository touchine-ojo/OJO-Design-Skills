# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want OJO Design Skills to be a welcoming, constructive project for everyone. Participation in this community requires respect for differing experience levels, design perspectives, identities, and technical backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Critique ideas and artifacts, not people.
- Give credit for work, references, and examples that are not your own.
- Respect reasonable requests for privacy and professional boundaries.
- Help keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith review behavior.
- Plagiarizing or sharing material you do not have the right to contribute.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently limit participation when behavior violates this code. To report a concern privately, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the relevant issue, pull request, or discussion and ask for maintainer review. Please include links, dates, and any context that will help us assess the situation.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is not tolerated.
